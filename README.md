<h2>What I Bring to a Product</h2>

I believe good product design starts with understanding the problem and exploring practical solutions. I follow a structured design process, combining research, strategy, creativity, and collaboration to create intuitive experiences that solve real problems

* Research & Discovery (Discover)<br/>
Understanding user needs, business goals, market opportunities, and existing challenges to identify the right problems to solve.

* Product Thinking & Strategy (Define)<br/>
Defining problems, mapping user journeys, exploring opportunities, and aligning product decisions with user needs and business objectives.

* Ideation & AI-Assisted Exploration (Explore)<br/>
Exploring creative concepts, generating ideas, and using AI-assisted tools to speed up ideation and discover practical design solutions.

* UX/UI & Interaction Design (Design)<br/>
Creating information architecture, user flows, wireframes, high-fidelity interfaces, and interactive prototypes that deliver intuitive experiences.

* Design Systems & Validation (Validate)<br/>
Building reusable components, consistent design patterns, and scalable design systems while validating ideas through feedback and usability reviews.

* Collaboration & Delivery (Deliver)<br/>
Working closely with developers, product managers, and stakeholders to refine designs, ensure technical feasibility, and deliver implementation-ready solutions.
